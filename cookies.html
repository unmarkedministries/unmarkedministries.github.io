Cookie Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how unmarkedministries.org (the "Site") uses cookies and similar technologies. The short version: we don't use any.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They are often used to remember preferences, keep you signed in, or track visits for analytics and advertising.

Cookies we use

The Site does not set any cookies of its own, and it does not use analytics, advertising, social media tracking, or similar tools.

Third-party services

The Site relies on a few outside services to work. Based on how they are used here, they do not place cookies in your browser, but your browser does connect to them, and they may receive basic technical information such as your IP address:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site
  • Google Fonts, which provides the Site's typefaces
  • Web3Forms, which receives contact and giving form submissions when you choose to send one

Links to other websites, such as social media, are governed by those sites' own cookie policies once you visit them.

Your choices

Because the Site does not use cookies, there is nothing to accept or decline here. You can still control cookies for all websites through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

If we ever add tools that use cookies, we will update this policy, and ask for your consent where required, before doing so.
