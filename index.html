Why we exist

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

Unmarked Ministries exists to restore the identity, dignity, and wholeness of women survivors of sex trafficking.

Our Vision

We envision a world where no woman is defined by what was done to her, where survivors are seen as whole, worthy, and free. We are building a network of faith-rooted healing resources, licensed professionals, and compassionate partners working together to offer every survivor a place to begin again.

The meaning of the pearl

A pearl is formed when something that never belonged is covered, layer by layer, until only beauty remains.

She was always precious. What was done to her was never part of her worth. The open shell is a safe place, a place of protection where healing happens, and where a woman steps out of hiding and into the light, seen and free.

What we do

Healing that goes deeper than skin

Free tattoo removal

Professional laser removal of branded or forced markings, provided at no cost by licensed laser technicians and medical professionals.

Wraparound care

We connect survivors with counseling, trauma-informed support, spiritual care, housing advocacy, and a community that stays.

Partnership and awareness

We partner with churches, shelters, medical providers, and advocates, and help our community recognize and respond to victims of sex trafficking.

How we'll thank you

Every partner is thanked personally within 48 hours and kept close all year with monthly updates and quarterly impact reports. Recognition is always offered, never required, and anyone may give anonymously.

  • Your name on our permanent Founding 120 wall and website
  • A numbered founding partner certificate, such as "Partner 37 of 120"
  • An "I'm One of the 120" badge to share
  • For businesses, your name or logo on our partner page and at events

Celebrated on our social media

With your permission, we'll welcome you publicly on Facebook and Instagram.

  • A personal welcome post when you claim your seat
  • A shout-out in our seat countdown, such as "Welcome, partner 37 of 120"
  • Business spotlight posts that tag your business and share what you do
  • A thank-you to every founding partner at our Founding 120 celebration

Prefer to stay behind the scenes? Just let us know, and we'll keep your gift private.

We will never share a survivor's name, photo, or story. Updates share numbers and milestones only.
