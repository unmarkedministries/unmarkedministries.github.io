About Unmarked Ministries

Unmarked Ministries exists to bring healing, freedom, and restored identity to survivors of sex trafficking. Through partnerships with licensed laser technicians and medical professionals, we provide professional tattoo removal services, erasing the marks of exploitation at no cost to survivors. We walk alongside each woman as she reclaims her body, her dignity, and her God-given future.

Unmarked Ministries provides free tattoo and branding removal for survivors of sex trafficking, along with connection to counseling, housing advocacy, job support and community. We are raising $120,000 for laser equipment and startup costs. Our goal is 120 partners giving $1,000 each, and gifts of any size are welcome.
