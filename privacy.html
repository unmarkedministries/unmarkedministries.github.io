Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Unmarked Ministries, Inc. ("Unmarked Ministries," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through unmarkedministries.org (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we collect only what you choose to send us, and we never sell it.

Our promise to survivors. We will never publish, sell, or share a survivor's name, photo, story, or any information that could identify her. Referral information is used only to arrange care and is shared only with the licensed professionals and advocates directly involved, and only as needed.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form or giving form, we receive the information you enter, which may include your name, organization, mailing address, phone number, email address, gift amount and type, payment method preference, recognition preference, typed signature, and any message you write.

Referrals. If you make a confidential referral, please share only first names or initials through the website. We follow up privately to talk through next steps.

Information collected automatically. The Site does not use analytics, advertising tools, or tracking pixels, and it does not set its own cookies. Like nearly all websites, the services that deliver the Site may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and to keep the Site running. See "Service providers" below.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your messages and questions
  • To process, record, and acknowledge your gift, including providing a written acknowledgment for tax purposes
  • To send updates and impact reports to partners who have given or asked to hear from us
  • To recognize partners publicly (for example, on our Partners page or social media), only with your permission
  • To arrange care for survivors referred to us
  • To meet legal, accounting, and charitable-registration obligations

How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us operate the Site and receive form submissions (listed below), who may use it only to provide their service
  • With licensed professionals and advocates directly involved in a survivor's care, when needed to arrange that care
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety of any person
  • Publicly, for partner recognition, only if you have told us that is okay

Service providers

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the Site and may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) provides the Site's typefaces; your browser requests fonts directly from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Web3Forms receives contact and giving form submissions and delivers them to our email inbox.
  • Google Workspace provides our email, where form submissions and correspondence are stored.

Each provider has its own privacy policy that governs its handling of data.

Gifts and payment information

The Site does not accept or store credit card or bank account numbers. Gifts are made by check or by bank transfer arranged privately by email. Please do not enter payment account numbers into any form on the Site.

How long we keep information

We keep donor and gift records as long as needed for our relationship with you and as required for tax, accounting, and legal purposes. We keep other messages only as long as they are useful to respond to you. Referral information is kept only as long as needed to arrange and provide care.

Your choices

  • You may ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • You may ask to give anonymously, to be removed from public recognition, or to stop receiving updates at any time.

To do any of these, email info@unmarkedministries.org.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you share with us, and the Site is served over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive details through web forms.

Children

The Site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
